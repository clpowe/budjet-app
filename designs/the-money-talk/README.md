# The Money Talk — brand exploration

Created October 6, 2026 from the two supplied logo references.

Three iterations of each logo, plus expansions of both original designs. Every board includes a main wordmark, two logo-icon explorations, and three app-thumbnail color variations.

## Recommendation

Soft Sage / Conversation is the strongest starting point for the brief. The rounded conversation motif makes communication visible; the dark forest ink and warm ivory feel grounded; the pale sage adds gentleness. My alternative is Quiet Blue / Conversation for a more reserved identity.

Use the stacked wordmark where there is room to read the name. Use the simplified mark on the home screen, in small navigation areas, and for favicons. The overlapping conversation bubbles in Soft Sage suggest two partners.

## Applying the direction to the app

- Give headings a readable serif with open counters and adequate stroke weight.
- Keep transaction amounts, form fields and supporting copy in a clear sans serif.
- Use warm light backgrounds and dark text, with muted color for secondary surfaces.
- Give conversation prompts and controls generous space.
- Keep thumbnail marks simple and bold enough to read at small sizes.
- Let welcoming language carry the tone: “Our plan,” “Next money talk,” and “What matters to us.”
